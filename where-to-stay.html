Stay Under the Stars

If you’re the outdoorsy type (or just want to keep the celebration going), our venue sits on 33 acres, and the owners have a campground available for anyone who wants to stay right on the property.

You’re welcome to pitch a tent, bring an RV, or just hang out under the stars after the reception.

The venue will have brunch the next morning, and we’d love to have you there. Checkout is at 12 PM.

If camping isn’t your thing, that’s totally okay. In addition to the hotels we’ve already shared, there are lots of hotels, motels, or Airbnbs between Rogers, Eureka Springs, and surrounding areas to make a weekend out of it.
